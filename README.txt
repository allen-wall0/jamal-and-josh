JOSH AND DOUG 3D, GOTY EDITION (Phase 3)

Host: upload every file and folder to the root of a static host such as GitHub Pages. Open index.html.
Campaign: Street, Warehouse, The Perimeter (stealth), Yard, The Escape (vehicle chase), Rail Yard, The Rooftops (parkour) into a Chris duel, Control Facility (Warhead Titan), endings, Wesker bonus fight.
Side content in Level Select: Duel: Jill, Case 1: The People vs. Doug (courtroom), plus replays of every chapter.
Melee duels: LMB light combo, hold LMB heavy, RMB block (tap just before a hit to parry), Space roll, Q staff spin when the spirit bar is full, F finisher on a staggered boss.
Parkour: WASD relative to camera, Shift sprint, Space jump, C slide (and roll when landing from high), run into walls to mantle.
Courtroom: Press for more info, Present the right evidence from the Court Record at the contradicting statement. Wrong evidence costs the Judge's patience.
Other controls: Esc pause, Q focus, P photo mode, E shove / takedown, F box (stealth), G throw can (stealth). Driving: W/S/A/D, Space drift, Shift boost.
Options: graphics preset, brightness, interface size, QTE assist. If the screen is black add ?gfx=retro to the URL.
